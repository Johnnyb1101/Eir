# Review one slide

## Objective checks

- Return one row per required point, using its numbered ID. Check the outline title as well as the objective. A named sequence, acronym, or list requires all of its components.
- Mark a point covered only if the bullets or speaker notes teach it. A mention, a title, or an acknowledgment that a point is missing is insufficient.
- For a covered point, copy at least two contiguous words from exactly one bullet or note. Never quote the slide title as evidence of teaching. Do not join passages with ellipses. For a whole title or objective taught across several units, choose one representative excerpt after checking every part. Mark the point missing or unclear if any required part is unaddressed.

## Content checks

- Return one row for every content unit, including the title. Use the exact ID from the prompt. Review the unit on its own even when the notes qualify wording on the slide face.
- Mark a unit supported only when the cited sources support every claim, including each item in a list. Supply as many exact source excerpts as needed. Use only chunk IDs in the cited list. Quote at least two contiguous words from one cited source per excerpt, without ellipses or paraphrases.
- Assess claim strength separately. Preserve the source's uncertainty, negation, scope, and conditions. Check that shorthand such as "=", "confirms", or "rules out" does not turn a possible finding into a diagnosis or a conditional recommendation into a general rule.
- An independent teaching detail can appear in notes alone. A bullet that names one supported finding or trigger can be brief when it does not present itself as an exhaustive list. Keep any qualification needed to make the bullet itself accurate on the slide face.
- Use no_claim and not_applicable only for a descriptive title, nonclinical prompt, or teaching direction with no evidence claim. Explain why no evidence applies and return an empty evidence list.
- Report discrepancies in numbers, doses, thresholds, or conditions, and disagreements between bullets and speaker notes. Include any problem that is not already explained by an objective or content row in additional_problems.
